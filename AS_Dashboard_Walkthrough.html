TOUR STEP 1 OF 6
Welcome to your dashboard
This is what you'll open at the start of every single shift. The green LIVE indicator in the top right means the data is real-time — it updates as calls happen. Click ↺ Refresh at any time to pull the latest numbers.
1 / 6
TOUR STEP 2 OF 6
The 5 team KPI cards
Dials, Bookings, Dials/Booking, Contact Rate, and Manual vs Predictive — team totals for the day, each checked against a target. "Beat it ✓" in green means the team is ahead. Watch the banner underneath too — it plain-English summarises where the team stands right now.
2 / 6
TOUR STEP 3 OF 6
Reading the leaderboard colours
Every agent gets their own row. Booked and Dials/Booking are colour coded: green means you're hitting target, gold means you're borderline, and red means it needs attention. A high Dials/Booking number (like 53) means you're burning a lot of dials for each booking — that's the one to watch.
3 / 6
TOUR STEP 4 OF 6
Hourly Dials & Pace vs Target
The Hourly Dials chart shows your activity shape across the day — useful for spotting a slow patch before it costs you the afternoon. Pace vs Target is a simple gauge: where you are right now against where the team needs to land by end of day. If the bar isn't filling in by early afternoon, that's your signal to pick up the pace.
4 / 6
TOUR STEP 5 OF 6
Manual vs Predictive — know which mode you're in
You'll dial in two modes: Manual (you click to dial one lead at a time — usually reschedules and warm follow-ups) and Predictive (the dialer auto-dials through the queue). The card shows how many of each you've done. If Manual is unusually high, check you're not sitting on a queue that should be running predictive.
5 / 6
TOUR STEP 6 OF 6 — COMPLETE
Your dashboard is a mirror ✓
What you put in is exactly what it shows back. A weak Dials/Booking number or a red Booked cell always trace back to the same thing — something upstream needs attention. The data doesn't lie, and it doesn't judge. It just shows you where to focus. Use it every single day — not just when things are going wrong.
